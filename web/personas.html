Personas

A cat and a dog, drawn live

Both animals are drawn frame by frame from about thirty numbers, at the resolution your screen actually asks for. There are no sprite sheets and no stock art, so there is nothing to pixelate — and every pose is sampled finely enough that the motion has no steps in it.

The cat

ginger tabby, white chest and socks

Sits and breathes, blinks, wanders off across the stage, grooms, curls up. Click her and she answers.

The dog

tan, floppy ears, long legs

Stands on a deep chest and hocked back legs, trots off, flops down to sleep, asks about walks. Click him and he answers.

Each stage renders its own library in the background: a coarse pass appears first at full resolution, then the same clips are re-sampled at more frames a second and swapped in. Behaviour is chosen the way it is on the desktop: resting weighted heavily, movement occasionally, and stillness left alone.
