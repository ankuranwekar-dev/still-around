Let's get your pet on screen.

Add a few photos and Still Around draws your cat or dog. It takes about a minute, and it all happens on this Mac.

  1. 1

    Add a few photos

    Four easy ones — their face, their side, sitting, and their tail. One short video works too.

  2. 2

    Check the likeness

    You'll see them drawn straight away. Click their eye to get the colour right, and nudge anything else that looks off.

  3. 3

    They move in

    Your pet lands on the desktop and potters about up there for as long as you like.

Your photos never leave this Mac. There is no account and no upload — they are read here and forgotten. You can also make one on the website instead.

Still Around has no window of its own. It lives in the menu bar — click the paw icon up there any time.

Say hello to your pet.

They are on your desktop now. Drag them anywhere along the floor, or give them a poke.

To add another, change the size, or send them away for a while, click the paw icon in your menu bar.
